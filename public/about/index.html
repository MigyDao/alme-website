Why A Likkle More Eco?

Practical solutions, built around real needs.

A Jamaica-based design and build business creating practical, more sustainable solutions for spaces, production and land.

Build for Useful Life

We design around the client’s needs, environment, budget and resources already available. Where practical, we prefer repair, reuse, repurposing and recycling before replacement, while balancing those choices with durability, repairability, safety, function and lifecycle value.

The practical questions

What already exists? What resources are available? How will it be maintained? Can it adapt? What happens later?

Where we’re headed

ALME is starting with the practical design, fabrication, growing and property capabilities we can deliver well today. Over time, we aim to develop those capabilities into a network of locally adapted systems that help communities meet everyday needs with better use of materials, skills, knowledge and local resources.

Future systems will be described as future systems until they have actually been built, tested and validated.

Rooted in Jamaica

Physical projects and on-site services are undertaken where practical. Digital design and consultation services can also be delivered internationally.

Design

Understand the need, constraints, materials, spatial relationships and fabrication requirements.

Build

Turn useful designs into physical results through fabrication, installation and coordination where appropriate.

Grow

Apply the same systems thinking to land, gardens, food-production infrastructure and resilience.

Practical ideas are better when they become useful results.
