What would you like help with?

You can ask about Product Design & Development; Infrastructure Design & Build; Project Coordination, Property Care & Systems Support; or ALME Products & Equipment. If you are unsure, describe the problem in your email or WhatsApp message.

Property Care & Operational Oversight

Periodic site checks, grounds tidying, light maintenance and minor fixes within competency, issue documentation, contractor coordination and improvement planning.

Local · On-site. Tell us the property location, support needed, preferred visit cadence and timing. Access arrangements, responsibilities, safety, specialist needs and pricing are confirmed during scoping.

Property care and operational oversight does not include tenant management, rental administration, property sales, financial management or regulated property-management functions. Licensed or specialist work is referred to or coordinated with appropriate providers.

  • Who will use or approve the result

What happens after you reach out?

  1. Inquiry review: we look at the problem, location, timing and fit.
  2. Clarification and discovery: we ask for missing photos, dimensions or constraints.
  3. Assessment: remote or site-based depending on the project.
  4. Scope and quote: deliverables, assumptions and price are defined.
  5. Approval and deposit: cash-consuming work begins after the required deposit.
  6. Design, build, coordination, handover and support according to the agreed scope.

Service area

Based in Jamaica. Physical and on-site work is considered based on location and logistics. Digital design and consultation services are available internationally.
