How we can work together

Client-Directed

You know what you want and need help turning it into a workable result.

Collaborative

We develop the solution together around your priorities and constraints.

Solution-Led

Bring us the problem and give us room to propose a practical way forward.

  1. Tell us about the problem, photos, approximate dimensions and location.
  2. Clarify requirements, constraints and useful options.
  3. Define scope, materials, labour, logistics and other requirements.
  4. Build, coordinate, deliver and support as appropriate.
