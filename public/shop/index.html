Growing & Garden

Custom Compost Systems

Made to Order · Custom Order

Custom compost systems for homes, gardens and small properties, with sizing and layout adapted to the site. Airflow, access and maintenance are considered alongside material availability, lifespan, repairability and budget. Systems can be integrated into a wider garden or growing setup where appropriate.

The modular three-bay compost design remains in development and has not been field-tested.
