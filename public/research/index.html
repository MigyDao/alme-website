Internal-use V1 · In development

Local-First 3D Sketchpad

A lightweight desktop 3D sketching tool being developed around ALME’s own design workflow: fast enough for early spatial thinking, structured enough for AI-assisted editing, and usable without a mandatory cloud account or internet connection.

Its role is the space between a rough sketch and detailed CAD: quickly establish orientation, proportion, clearance, adjacency and basic dimensions, then hand the work downstream to more specialized tools when needed.

Local-first · Structured scene editing · Practical 3D alignment · Interoperable workflow

See how 3D modelling supports ALME’s design-to-build process →

Why build a tool instead of only using one?

ALME’s R&D starts from real workflow friction. Sketchpad began as an internal attempt to make early-stage 3D alignment faster and easier to inspect, modify and hand off between human and AI workflows. Its value is being tested first through actual ALME and maker projects before any broader product decision.

Evidence state: working internal-use V1; broader product fit remains under evaluation.
